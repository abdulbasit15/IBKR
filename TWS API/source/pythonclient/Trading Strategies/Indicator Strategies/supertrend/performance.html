The headline

v1 is the production line. Its stack improves at every stage in backtest and it carries the _safe_sleep() Gateway-restart reliability fix. The regime gate is the biggest single win and roughly halves max drawdown; partial-take-profit adds a further edge on top. (The sibling v2 branch swaps partial-TP for a mean-reversion fade that backtests flat-to-negative at its defaults.)

Timeframe matters: gains concentrate on 15m and 1h; 30m is the weak timeframe in every series — hence the deployed strategies run on 15m.

Why each filter is (or isn't) on

  • Raw Supertrend alone is marginal — whipsaws in chop (win rate ~30%, PF often < 1.1).
  • RSI > 50 / < 50 momentum helps win rate (+6–10 pts) and drawdown, mostly on 30m/1h; MACD similar but mixed.
  • DEMA / ADX gates mostly delay entries (redundant with Supertrend); DEMA on top of the regime gate is ≈ a wash — kept on for a small drawdown benefit.
  • Regime gate (stand-aside in chop) is the biggest win and supersedes the always-on RSI/MACD gates when enabled (does not override DEMA).

Verdict

Deploy on 15m (DEMA200 + regime stand-aside + partial-TP). Every stage backtests positive on the deployed timeframe, and it has the _safe_sleep() Gateway-restart fix. Avoid 30m (the weak timeframe in every series above).
