## ADR-based stop (2026-10-04)
`apply_adr_stop(trigger, raw_stop, adr_pct, use_adr, mult)` in qm_long_backtest.py (imported by qm_bot). Returns
the TIGHTER of the structural stop and `trigger − mult×ADR` → caps stop distance at ~1 ADR (Qullamaggie). Wired
into all 3 bot analyzers (breakout/parabolic/ep via p.adr_stop/p.adr_stop_mult from scan_setups & scan_ep) AND the
backtest (`--adr-stop`/`--adr-stop-mult`, applied in backtest_symbol after next-open entry). Config `adr_stop`
(false default) + `adr_stop_mult` (1.0) in qm_bot.json/dist/qm_live.json. Effect: risk stays 1%, position/notional
~doubles (CVI 11%→5.4% stop, 158→330 sh). Trade-off = more stop-outs; small-sample backtest on the armed names:
baseline 46.9% win/PF2.39/+$10.8k vs adr-stop 42.4%/PF1.93/+$8.6k (tighter stop hurt slightly there). Off by default.

## Run anytime (entry window)
The bot is meant to run 24/7 (`run_qm.ps1` supervisor). It self-schedules and only ACTS at ET times.
`entry_window` (default `["09:40","11:00"]`) gates entries+EP: they fire ONLY inside that window, so
starting the bot after hours or mid-afternoon places NO late/after-hours entry orders — it waits for the
next window. `manage_time` (15:50) and reconnect are always-on regardless of the window. (Before this,
a fresh start after 09:40 ran a catch-up entry scan that placed after-hours DAY orders.)

## Architecture (bot)
Swing bot on **daily bars**. Two daily jobs driven by an ET clock in `run_loop()`:
1. **Entry (~09:40 ET, `entry_time`)** → `reconcile()` then `run_entries()`:
   - `scan_setups(cfg)` fetches the universe (Yahoo/Stooq, 10 threads) and runs the enabled analyzers from
     `ANALYZERS = {"breakout": analyze_breakout, "parabolic_long": analyze_parabolic_long}`; one best
     candidate per symbol, ranked by `score`.
   - Fills only free slots = `max_positions − (live ∪ state ∪ resting buy-stops)`.
   - Sizing: `qty = floor(capital × risk_per_trade_pct / (trigger − stop))`, capped by `max_position_pct`.
     `capital` = IB `NetLiquidation` unless `capital_override`.
   - `place_entry()` sends a **native IB bracket**: parent `StopOrder("BUY", qty, trigger)` (transmit False,
     DAY) + child `StopOrder("SELL", qty, stop)` (parentId set, transmit True, GTC). A filled position is
     never unprotected. Order ids + setup saved to state.
2. **Manage (~15:50 ET, `manage_time`)** → `reconcile()` then `run_manage()` per held name:
   - `days_held` from the daily bars vs `entry_date`.
   - After `partial_days`: `_sell(half)` at MKT, `partial_done=True`, `_move_stop(break-even)` (cancel+replace child).
   - Trail: if last close `< SMA(trail_sma)` → `_sell(remaining)` + `_cancel_stop`, drop from state.
   - Hard stop is the resting bracket child (handled server-side; if IB shows the name flat, state is cleared).

`reconcile()` drops state for names no longer on IB, adopts untracked live positions (trail-manage only),
and trusts IB for the live share count.

**Reconnect resilience (added 2026-09-28, mirrors supertrend_bot).** The bot survives an IB Gateway restart or
a competing login bumping its API client. `_connect_once()` opens a **fresh `IB()` per attempt** (reconnecting the
dropped object fails with clientId-in-use). `_wire_events()` hooks `errorEvent` (1100 lost / 1101-1102 restored →
`self._conn_ok`) + `disconnectedEvent`. `ensure_connected()` is called at the top of every `run_loop` tick: if the
socket is down it retries **5s ×3 then every `reconnect_backoff_sec` (60s), indefinitely** (swing bot, no daily
give-up), and on success re-runs `reconcile()`. `_safe_sleep()` replaces raw `ib.sleep()` in the loop so a socket
drop during the heartbeat can't kill the loop. The loop's `except (asyncio.CancelledError, Exception)` (Cancelled
is a BaseException `except Exception` misses) tears down the dead client and lets the next tick reconnect;
`KeyboardInterrupt` is re-raised for a clean Ctrl+C. Positions rest on their server-side bracket stops during any
outage. Config: `reconnect_backoff_sec` (default 60).

## Strategy logic (exact)
**Breakout** (`analyze_breakout` in bot; `breakout_signal` in backtest): uptrend `close>SMA20>SMA50`;
3-month gain in `[min_move_pct, max_move_pct]`; tight base over `cons_days` (range ≤ `max_base_range_pct`);
within `near_high_pct` of 52-wk high; `ADR% ≥ min_adr_pct`; `$vol ≥ min_dollar_vol_m`; **not yet broken out**
(bot places a buy-stop at base-high; backtest fires on the confirmed close-breakout and enters next open).
Stop = base low. Skip if `(trigger−stop)/trigger > max_risk_frac`.

**Parabolic long** (`analyze_parabolic_long` / `parabolic_long_signal`): find the parabolic peak in
`pl_lookback`; require run-up ≥ `pl_runup`% over `pl_runup_days` into it; crash ≥ `pl_drop`% within
`pl_drop_days` to a low; bounce still fresh (`i − low_idx ≤ pl_bounce_within`); price ≤ low ×
(1 + `pl_max_bounce`%). Entry = first range-break up (bot: buy-stop at recent swing high; backtest: today's
close > prior day high → next open). Stop = crash low. **Rare by design.**

**EP** live (`scan_ep` in bot): confirm an earnings catalyst via `earnings.recent_earnings()` (Nasdaq calendar,
prev-day after-hours + today pre-market); require today's open gap ≥ `ep_gap`% (today_open vs prev_close from the
daily fetch), prior-6m gain ≤ `ep_max_prior`%, liquidity, and a volume-so-far surge; pull `fetch_intraday_5m()`
for the opening range → trigger = ORH (first `ep_or_bars`×5-min), stop = ORL. Optional `ep_min_surprise` beat gate.
Runs at `ep_entry_time` (its own schedule slot in `run_loop`, before the daily scan). Backtest EP (`ep_signal`) is
a gap+volume+not-extended proxy (no earnings feed in the backtest path).

**Shared exits (both/all):** initial stop at the breakout/crash low → after `partial_days` sell
`partial_fraction` + break-even → trail on `trail_sma`, exit on first close below.

## Known limitations / gotchas
1. **Not gateway-tested.** Bracket fills, stop cancel/replace on partials, and reconciliation need a real
   paper run. Watch: child-stop qty after a partial; DAY vs GTC on parent/child; adopted positions.
2. **Daily-bar approximation of an intraday method.** Qullamaggie enters on 1/5/60-min opening-range highs;
   this uses buy-stops on daily levels. Real edge also includes discretionary base quality + market regime.
   A fair-universe backtest of the mechanical breakout was **negative** — treat the bot as a disciplined
   executor, not a proven edge.
3. **No market-regime filter yet.** Highest-value next add: only take new longs when SPY/QQQ > 50/200-day MA
   (he stops buying breakouts in bad tape). Would gate `run_entries()` only.
4. **Universe cache in frozen exe** writes into the temp `_MEIxxx` dir → persists for the process lifetime but
   is re-downloaded on restart. Harmless (once/day) but note it.
5. **Parabolic long is rare** in large caps — needs a genuine 50%+ multi-day crash. Point the universe at
   small caps to see any. Riskiest of the three (catching a falling knife).
6. **EP timing is intraday & data-dependent.** It needs the market open (today's gap + a 5-min opening range) and
   the Nasdaq earnings calendar; run at `ep_entry_time`. Yahoo 5-min only covers recent days. The Nasdaq `surprise`
   field is used as-is (units not normalized) for the optional beat gate. Test at a real open.
