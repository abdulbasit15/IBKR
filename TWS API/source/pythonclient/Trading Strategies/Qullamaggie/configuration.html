Universe / screener (what type of stocks it scans)

The scanner looks at US-listed common stocks only — Qullamaggie's actual hunting ground: small-to-mid-cap, liquid, priced above $10. It is not mega-caps, penny stocks, or funds.

The universe (from universe.py)

  • Source: the full Nasdaq / NYSE / AMEX common-stock list from api.nasdaq.com (~7,000 names).
  • Kept: plain tickers only (^[A-Z]{1,5}$), US common stock.
  • Excluded: ETFs, funds, warrants, units, rights, preferreds (filtered by name).
  • Cap / price band:
    • min_cap_m — default $300M+ market cap
    • max_cap_m — $20B ceiling in the bot config (0 = no ceiling)
    • min_price — $10+
    • min_dollar_vol_m — $5M+ average daily dollar volume

So the base pool is roughly small-to-mid-cap, liquid, $10+ US common stocks — deliberately Qullamaggie's hunting ground (not mega-caps, not sub-$10 illiquid junk, no ETFs).

Then each setup narrows it further at scan time

breakout (defaults shown)

  • Up 30–300% over ~3 months (min_move_pct 30 / max_move_pct 300)
  • In an uptrend: close > SMA20 > SMA50
  • Tight base: last 10-day range ≤ 20% (cons_days 10 / max_base_range_pct 20)
  • Within 15% of the 52-wk high (near_high_pct 15)
  • ADR% ≥ 3 (min_adr_pct) · $vol ≥ $5M (min_dollar_vol_m)
  • Not yet broken out

parabolic_long (defaults shown)

  • Ran up ≥ 50% into the peak (pl_runup 50 over pl_runup_days 20)
  • Crashed ≥ 50% within 15 days (pl_drop 50 / pl_drop_days 15)
  • Basing within 5 days of the crash low (pl_bounce_within 5)
  • Price within 30% of that low, not chased (pl_max_bounce 30)

ep (defaults shown)

  • Confirmed earnings catalyst (Nasdaq calendar)
  • Gaps up ≥ 10% at the open (ep_gap 10)
  • Not already extended: prior-6m ≤ 50% (ep_max_prior 50)
  • Opening range = first 1×5-min bar (ep_or_bars 1) · volume surge
  • Runs at the open 09:40 ET (ep_entry_time)

pullback (defaults shown)

  • Same leader filter as breakout (3-month move, uptrend, liquid)
  • Pulled back to a rising pb_ma (10)-day MA — a recent low within pb_near (2%)
  • Now resuming: buy-STOP at the prior day's high (the reclaim)
  • Not chased: trigger within pb_max_ext (5%) of the MA
  • Joins leaders it missed on the first breakout (the late-entry gap)

So the universe is the haystack; the setup filters find the ~handful of needles each day.

Two different defaults. The live bot (qm_bot.json) ships with "universe":"us" → it scans the whole filtered US market. The standalone screener (qm_screener.py) defaults to "builtin" (a curated ~72-name list) unless you pass --universe us. Point either at small caps (min_cap_m 300, max_cap_m 5000) to lean into his sweet spot.

Overview — what the bot does

A swing bot that automates the long setups from Qullamaggie's "3 timeless setups". It scans the broad US market once per day for stocks in a strong momentum profile, places buy-stop entries at the breakout trigger with a protective stop attached (native IB bracket), sizes each to risk 1% of capital, holds at most 10 names, and manages exits by his rules (partial + break-even + moving-average trail).

Timeframe & cadence

  • Daily bars for all signals (swing horizon, multi-day holds).
  • Scan runs once/day at entry_time (default 09:40 ET, just after the opening range).
  • Manage runs once/day at manage_time (default 15:50 ET, near the close).
  • Between those it just heartbeats every poll_seconds (60s); the IB bracket handles intraday stop fills.

Data sources (no PyPI at runtime)

  • Universe list: api.nasdaq.com screener (cached to universe_us_raw.csv).
  • Daily bars: Yahoo primary, Stooq fallback, via urllib.
  • EP: Nasdaq earnings calendar + Yahoo 5-min intraday (opening range).
  • Account equity, positions, orders: IBKR via ib_async.

How candidates are ranked (quality)

Everything that passes the filters is scored and the best fill the slots. The breakout score is a weighted blend of the factors Qullamaggie emphasises (tunable via quality_weights):

  • Relative strength — 3-month return in excess of the Nasdaq (QQQ) — the index Qullamaggie watches (rs_benchmark, defaults to regime_symbol). Leaders beat the market, not just rise.
  • ADR sweet-spot — rewards ADR%, but capped so the most volatile junk doesn't auto-win.
  • Base tightness — a smaller consolidation range scores higher.
  • Volume dry-up — lighter volume in the base vs the prior run (accumulation / contraction).
  • Proximity to the 52-week high, trend alignment (above the 200-day SMA), and an orderly base (higher lows).

EP ranks by gap% + earnings surprise + opening relative-volume + ADR; parabolic_long by crash-depth + ADR + bounce freshness.

Market regime filter (his rule)

Momentum setups only work in an up-trending market — "breakouts don't exist in a falling market." Before entries the bot checks the index: if its short MA is above its long MA the regime is GOOD and trend entries run; if it's below, the regime is BAD and trend entries fully stop for the day (exits/management stay active).

  • Rule: regime_symbol (QQQ) regime_fast_ma (10) vs regime_slow_ma (20).
  • Blocks breakout + ep. parabolic_long is exempt (it's a counter-trend bounce) via regime_exempt_setups.
  • Toggle with regime_filter. Fail-open: if the index data can't be fetched it does not block.

When it opens

  • Regime gate first: in a bad market, no new breakout/EP entries at all (parabolic exempt).
  • Quality-driven selection with a ceiling: candidates are ranked by score; the bot takes those that clear the min_quality_score floor, up to max_positions (ceiling, default 20) per strategy. On a weak day far fewer qualify — concentration over count. A name is never entered in two strategies at once; an empty scan is skipped.
  • Each entry is a DAY buy-stop at the trigger; it only fills if price actually breaks out that session, otherwise it expires and is re-scanned next day.
  • Morning cancel-and-refresh: at the start of the entry window the bot cancels any leftover unfilled entry (BUY-STOP) orders and clears their pending state, so each day re-arms from a clean slate on the fresh ranking + today's levels (works even if entry_tif is GTC). Protective SELL stops of filled positions are never touched.
  • Size = floor(capital × risk_per_trade_pct / (trigger − stop)), capped so one name ≤ max_position_pct of capital.
Aggregate risk. Each of the four setups has its OWN max_positions cap (10) and risks 1% of its OWN sleeve per trade. At the ceiling that's 10 × 4 × 1% = up to ~$40k risk across $400k of sleeves if every position stopped at once. The min_quality_score floor is what keeps you well below that (you rarely hit the ceiling) — set it. Otherwise lower max_positions/risk_per_trade_pct or run fewer setups.

When it exits (per-setup, matches his method)

  • Hard stop (always resting, server-side): the initial protective stop at the base / opening-range / crash low.
  • Breakout / EP / pullback — trend trail: sell partial_fraction (half) into strength — when price is ≥ partial_ext_adr (4) ADRs above the trail MA (a climax, can fire early), or after partial_days (5) held as a fallback — both only once up ≥ partial_min_r (0.5R); then handle the remaining stop per be_stop_r (default -99 = trail-only: keep the original stop, don't snap to break-even — backtest shows break-even nearly halves returns); and trail the remainder on that setup's MA, exiting on the first daily close below it. Trail length is per-setup (trail_sma_by_setup): breakout 20, EP 50, pullback 20.
  • Parabolic long — take-profit into the MA: it's a fast mean-reversion bounce, so instead of trailing it sells into strength: half when price reaches the 10-day MA (then stop → break-even), the rest at the 20-day MA (target_ma2). A stall is time-exited after target_max_days (15). The crash-low hard stop guards the downside throughout.

Exit style per setup is set by exit_mode_by_setup (trail vs target_ma) and the MA lengths by trail_sma_by_setup / target_ma2.

Setup parameters

"setups" selects which long setups are active, e.g. ["breakout","parabolic_long","ep","pullback"]. Each runs independently with its own cap, sleeve and quality floor.

Breakout momentum continuation

cons_daysConsolidation/base lookback in days (default 10).
max_base_range_pctMax % range of the base (default 20) — how tight the coil must be.
near_high_pctMax % below the 52-week high (default 15).
min_adr_pctMinimum Average Daily Range % (default 3) — his volatility gauge.
breakout_buffer_pctBuffer above the base high for the buy-stop trigger (default 0.1%).
max_risk_fracSkip a setup if (trigger−stop)/trigger exceeds this (default 0.25) — caps per-share risk.
adr_stop / adr_stop_multADR-based stop (default off). When on, the stop is the TIGHTER of the structural low (base/crash/opening-range) and trigger − adr_stop_mult×ADR — i.e. capped at ~1 ADR, Qullamaggie-style. Risk stays 1%, but the tighter stop means a larger position / notional. Trade-off: more frequent stop-outs on noise (on a small sample backtest it slightly lowered win-rate/PF). Applies to breakout, EP, and parabolic; same flags exist in the backtest (--adr-stop).

Parabolic long bounce after a crash

KeyMeaning
pl_lookbackWindow (days) to locate the parabolic peak (default 40).
pl_runup / pl_runup_daysMin % run-up into the peak (50) over this many days (20) — confirms it WAS parabolic.
pl_drop / pl_drop_daysMin % crash from peak to low (50) within this many days (15) — a fast crash.

Parabolic longs are rare by design. A genuine 50%+ multi-day crash of a former parabolic is uncommon, especially in liquid large-caps. Expect few/none in a large-cap band; point the universe at small caps (min_cap_m 300, max_cap_m 5000) to surface them.

Pullback MA continuation

A strong leader (same quality universe as breakout) in an established uptrend that pulls back to a rising 10-day MA and resumes. Buy-STOP at the prior day's high (the reclaim), stop = the pullback swing low. Lets the bot join leaders it missed on the first breakout (the late-entry gap). Scored on the same scale as breakout, so its min_quality_score_by_setup.pullback floor and strategy_capital_by_setup.pullback sleeve work the same way. Trend-following → the regime filter blocks it (not exempt). Backtest: --setup pullback or --setup bp (breakout+pullback).

KeyMeaning
pb_maReference MA the pullback must tag and that must be rising — 10 or 20 (default 10).
pb_lookbackBars to look back for a pullback tag, and the window for the swing-low stop (default 8).
pb_nearA recent low within this % of the MA counts as a pullback "tag" (default 2.0%).
pb_max_extSkip if the entry trigger is more than this % above the MA (default 5.0%) — don't chase, buy near the MA.
Runs with or without breakout. Add/remove "pullback" in "setups" independently. It gets its OWN cap of max_positions, its own sleeve and quality floor — e.g. ["pullback"] for pullback-only, or ["breakout","pullback"] to run both. 2-yr full-universe backtest: pullback-only beat breakout-only (PF 1.05 vs 0.97) — relative ranking only (mechanical harness + survivorship bias flatters dip-buying).

Exits & sizing

KeyMeaning
risk_per_trade_pctFraction of capital risked per ticker (default 0.01 = 1%).
max_positionsPer-strategy ceiling on open names (default 20). It's a cap, not a target — the min_quality_score floor decides how many are actually taken. A symbol held/pending in any strategy is never entered twice; an empty scan is skipped.
min_quality_score / min_quality_score_by_setupQuality floor — only enter candidates whose score clears the bar (0 = off → fill to the ceiling). Scores scale differently per setup, so prefer the per-setup map, e.g. {"breakout": 95}. Calibrate from the ranked-candidate logs / qm_entries_*.csv. This is the lever that makes selection quality-driven and keeps aggregate risk low.
quality_weightsOptional override of the breakout ranking weights (keys: rs, adr, tight, voldry, near, trend, hl). Merges over the defaults — omit to use them.
max_position_pctCap on any single position as a fraction of capital (default 0.30 — his overnight max).
partial_daysDays held before the time-based partial sell (default 5; his "3–5 days"). This is the fallback trigger when partial_ext_adr hasn't fired.
partial_min_rOnly take the partial if the position is up at least this many R (default 0.5). Guards against scaling out of a loser (and the break-even-above-market bug).
partial_ext_adrSell INTO STRENGTH (Qullamaggie's real rule). Fire the partial when the close is ≥ this many ADRs above the trail MA — a climax — which can trigger early (day 1+), not just on day 5. Default 4 (backtest sweet-spot; 3 trims too early and caps winners, 5+ too rare). 0 = off → pure time rule. Still gated by partial_min_r; the partial_days time rule remains the fallback. Same flag in the backtest (--partial-ext-adr).
be_stop_rWhere the stop goes AFTER the partial, in R from entry (R = entry − initial stop). -99 (default, trail-only) = don't reprice the stop — keep the original setup-low stop and let the per-setup MA trail catch up. 0 = break-even (entry). +0.5 = lock ½R profit (tighter). -0.5 = ½R below entry (looser). Backtest (2y): trail-only +$62.9k vs break-even +$31.0k — moving to break-even nearly halves returns (it stops you flat on a normal pullback, then the name resumes without you); the cost is a deeper drawdown. The remaining stop order is always resized to the leftover shares regardless. Applies to the trail setups (breakout/ep/pullback); parabolic keeps break-even after its TP1. Same flag in the backtest (--be-stop-r; use -99 for trail-only).
partial_fractionFraction sold at the partial (default 0.5 = half). The remaining stop is then handled per be_stop_r above.
trail_smaFallback trail MA if a setup isn't in trail_sma_by_setup (default 20).
trail_sma_by_setupPer-setup MA length. Default {breakout:20, ep:50, parabolic_long:10, pullback:20}. For trail setups it's the trailing MA; for parabolic it's the first take-profit MA.
exit_mode_by_setupPer-setup exit style: "trail" (breakout/ep/pullback — trail an MA) or "target_ma" (parabolic — take profit into the MA). Default picks target_ma for parabolic_long, trail otherwise.
target_ma2 / target_max_daysParabolic only: the 2nd take-profit MA (default 20) and a time-exit if the bounce stalls (default 15 days).

Going live & scaling up

Use the dedicated qm_live.json (ships separate from paper, so your paper config stays intact): it's paper:false, port:4001, $10k per strategy at 1% risk, a 5% daily-loss circuit breaker, and its own qm_live_state.json / logs_live / qm_live_trades.csv.

# one-time: set "account" to your live U-account in qm_live.json, build, start the LIVE gateway (4001)
.\qm.ps1                         # copies qm_live.json into dist too
.\run_qm_live.ps1               # supervisor: dist\qm_bot.exe --config qm_live.json --i-understand-live

# or one-shot / checks:
cd dist
.\qm_bot.exe --config qm_live.json --check
.\qm_bot.exe --config qm_live.json --i-understand-live --once

Scaling plan — once it performs well, raise two numbers in qm_live.json:

  • Capital: bump strategy_capital_by_setup (e.g. 10k → 25k → 50k per sleeve).
  • Risk: raise risk_per_trade_pct (e.g. 0.005 → 0.01). Dollar risk = sleeve × this.
  • Optionally start live with fewer setups (e.g. just ["breakout"]) and add EP/parabolic later.
Keep sleeves ≤ your real account. strategy_capital_by_setup is a FIXED notional the bot sizes off — it does not know your balance. If total sleeves exceed your live NetLiquidation you'll be over-leveraged / get margin rejects. On a $40k live account, keep the four sleeves ≤ ~$10k each.
