Qullamaggie LONG Bot — Configuration & Overview

JSON reference for qm_bot.json + how the bot scans, enters and exits. Long only — no shorts.

Paper-first, dry-run-first. Ships with paper:true (gateway port 4002) and dry_run:true (logs orders, places none). LIVE needs paper:false and the CLI flag --i-understand-live. No live account without LPL pre-clearance. Not financial advice.

Universe / screener (what type of stocks it scans)

The scanner looks at US-listed common stocks only — Qullamaggie's actual hunting ground: small-to-mid-cap, liquid, priced above $10. It is not mega-caps, penny stocks, or funds.

The universe (from universe.py)

  • Source: the full Nasdaq / NYSE / AMEX common-stock list from api.nasdaq.com (~7,000 names).
  • Kept: plain tickers only (^[A-Z]{1,5}$), US common stock.
  • Excluded: ETFs, funds, warrants, units, rights, preferreds (filtered by name).
  • Cap / price band:
    • min_cap_m — default $300M+ market cap
    • max_cap_m — $20B ceiling in the bot config (0 = no ceiling)
    • min_price — $10+
    • min_dollar_vol_m — $5M+ average daily dollar volume

So the base pool is roughly small-to-mid-cap, liquid, $10+ US common stocks — deliberately Qullamaggie's hunting ground (not mega-caps, not sub-$10 illiquid junk, no ETFs).

Then each setup narrows it further at scan time

breakout (defaults shown)

  • Up 30–300% over ~3 months (min_move_pct 30 / max_move_pct 300)
  • In an uptrend: close > SMA20 > SMA50
  • Tight base: last 10-day range ≤ 20% (cons_days 10 / max_base_range_pct 20)
  • Within 15% of the 52-wk high (near_high_pct 15)
  • ADR% ≥ 3 (min_adr_pct) · $vol ≥ $5M (min_dollar_vol_m)
  • Not yet broken out

parabolic_long (defaults shown)

  • Ran up ≥ 50% into the peak (pl_runup 50 over pl_runup_days 20)
  • Crashed ≥ 50% within 15 days (pl_drop 50 / pl_drop_days 15)
  • Basing within 5 days of the crash low (pl_bounce_within 5)
  • Price within 30% of that low, not chased (pl_max_bounce 30)

ep (defaults shown)

  • Confirmed earnings catalyst (Nasdaq calendar)
  • Gaps up ≥ 10% at the open (ep_gap 10)
  • Not already extended: prior-6m ≤ 50% (ep_max_prior 50)
  • Opening range = first 1×5-min bar (ep_or_bars 1) · volume surge
  • Runs at the open 09:40 ET (ep_entry_time)

So the universe is the haystack; the setup filters find the ~handful of needles each day.

Two different defaults. The live bot (qm_bot.json) ships with "universe":"us" → it scans the whole filtered US market. The standalone screener (qm_screener.py) defaults to "builtin" (a curated ~72-name list) unless you pass --universe us. Point either at small caps (min_cap_m 300, max_cap_m 5000) to lean into his sweet spot.

Overview — what the bot does

A swing bot that automates the long setups from Qullamaggie's "3 timeless setups". It scans the broad US market once per day for stocks in a strong momentum profile, places buy-stop entries at the breakout trigger with a protective stop attached (native IB bracket), sizes each to risk 1% of capital, holds at most 10 names, and manages exits by his rules (partial + break-even + moving-average trail).

Timeframe & cadence

  • Daily bars for all signals (swing horizon, multi-day holds).
  • Scan runs once/day at entry_time (default 09:40 ET, just after the opening range).
  • Manage runs once/day at manage_time (default 15:50 ET, near the close).
  • Between those it just heartbeats every poll_seconds (60s); the IB bracket handles intraday stop fills.

Data sources (no PyPI at runtime)

  • Universe list: api.nasdaq.com screener (cached to universe_us_raw.csv).
  • Daily bars: Yahoo primary, Stooq fallback, via urllib.
  • EP: Nasdaq earnings calendar + Yahoo 5-min intraday (opening range).
  • Account equity, positions, orders: IBKR via ib_async.

How candidates are ranked (quality)

Everything that passes the filters is scored and the best fill the slots. The breakout score is a weighted blend of the factors Qullamaggie emphasises (tunable via quality_weights):

  • Relative strength — 3-month return in excess of SPY (leaders beat the market, not just rise).
  • ADR sweet-spot — rewards ADR%, but capped so the most volatile junk doesn't auto-win.
  • Base tightness — a smaller consolidation range scores higher.
  • Volume dry-up — lighter volume in the base vs the prior run (accumulation / contraction).
  • Proximity to the 52-week high, trend alignment (above the 200-day SMA), and an orderly base (higher lows).

EP ranks by gap% + earnings surprise + opening relative-volume + ADR; parabolic_long by crash-depth + ADR + bounce freshness.

When it opens

  • Per-strategy slots: breakout, EP, and parabolic_long each fill up to max_positions (10) independently — up to 30 names total, 1% risk each. A name is never entered in two strategies at once. An empty scan for a strategy is skipped.
  • Each entry is a DAY buy-stop at the trigger; it only fills if price actually breaks out that session, otherwise it expires and is re-scanned next day.
  • Size = floor(capital × risk_per_trade_pct / (trigger − stop)), capped so one name ≤ max_position_pct of capital.
Aggregate risk. 10 per strategy × 3 strategies × 1% = up to ~30% of capital at risk if every position stopped out at once. Lower max_positions or risk_per_trade_pct, or run fewer setups, if that's more than you want exposed.

When it exits

  • Hard stop (always resting, server-side): the initial protective stop at the base/crash low.
  • Partial + break-even: after partial_days (5) held, sell partial_fraction (half) at market and move the stop to break-even.
  • Trail: exit the remainder on the first daily close below the trail_sma (10 or 20-day). Close-based, so intraday wicks don't shake you out.

How it runs (build & launch)

# Build the exe + copy config into dist\ (from this folder)
.\qm.ps1

# Sanity checks (no IB needed)
cd dist
.\qm_bot.exe --check          # validate config + imports
.\qm_bot.exe --scan-only      # print today's setups

# With IB Gateway running (paper on 4002):
.\qm_bot.exe --once           # one entry+manage cycle, then exit
.\qm_bot.exe                  # scheduled loop (leave running)

# Keep it always-on (relaunches on crash / gateway outage):
..\run_qm.ps1

From source instead of the exe: python qm_bot.py --scan-only, etc. Config/state live next to the exe (or the .py when run from source): qm_bot.json, qm_bot_state.json; logs go to logs/ (combined + per-setup) and per-setup trade CSVs (qm_trades_<setup>.csv) to the root.

Running in parallel with the other bots. All three can share the same IB Gateway (paper port 4002) at once — IB allows many API clients, each on a unique client_id:

Connection / safety

KeyMeaning
papertrue (default) → paper gateway. false → live, and ONLY if --i-understand-live is also passed.
dry_runtrue (default) → logs every order it would place, transmits nothing. Set false to arm.
host / portTWS/Gateway. 127.0.0.1 / 4002 (paper gateway; live gateway = 4001; TWS 7497 paper / 7496 live).
client_idAPI client id (default 50). Must be unique per IB connection — for running in parallel: Intraday Equity uses 30–33, Supertrend 40+, this bot the 50s. All three share one gateway (port 4002) fine, each on its own id.
accountAccount to trade. Blank → adopts the login's sole managed account.
reconnect_backoff_secSteady reconnect retry interval in seconds (default 60). The bot survives an IB Gateway restart / a competing login: it retries fast (5s ×3) then every this-many seconds, indefinitely, and re-reconciles state on reconnect. Positions rest on their server-side stops meanwhile.
capital_override0 → use live NetLiquidation for sizing. >0 → size off this fixed number instead.
trade_log_csvBase filename (next to the exe) for the trades log; the setup is inserted, giving one CSV per setup — qm_trades_breakout.csv, qm_trades_parabolic_long.csv, qm_trades_ep.csv. A row is appended as each leg closes (partial, break-even, trail, stop) in the Backtest Journal format, so python -m journal backtest ingests them. Default base qm_trades.csv. Logs land in logs/: a combined qm_bot_<date>.log plus a per-setup qm_<setup>_<date>.log for each setup.

Trade log columns: symbol, strat, entry_id, side, qty, entry_time_et, entry_price, exit_time_et, exit_price, reason, R_points, R_result, pnl. One row per exit leg; legs of one trade share entry_id (e.g. a PARTIAL leg + the final TRAIL/Stop leg).

Setup parameters

"setups" selects which long setups are active, e.g. ["breakout","parabolic_long"].

Breakout momentum continuation

KeyMeaning
min_move_pct / max_move_pctRequired 3-month gain band (30–300%). Upper bound skips already-parabolic names.
cons_daysConsolidation/base lookback in days (default 10).
max_base_range_pctMax % range of the base (default 20) — how tight the coil must be.
near_high_pctMax % below the 52-week high (default 15).
min_adr_pctMinimum Average Daily Range % (default 3) — his volatility gauge.
breakout_buffer_pctBuffer above the base high for the buy-stop trigger (default 0.1%).
max_risk_fracSkip a setup if (trigger−stop)/trigger exceeds this (default 0.25) — caps per-share risk.

Parabolic long bounce after a crash

KeyMeaning
pl_lookbackWindow (days) to locate the parabolic peak (default 40).
pl_runup / pl_runup_daysMin % run-up into the peak (50) over this many days (20) — confirms it WAS parabolic.
pl_drop / pl_drop_daysMin % crash from peak to low (50) within this many days (15) — a fast crash.
pl_bounce_withinOnly enter within N days of the crash low (default 5) — fresh bounce.
pl_max_bounceMax % above the crash low to still enter (default 30) — don't chase.
Parabolic longs are rare by design. A genuine 50%+ multi-day crash of a former parabolic is uncommon, especially in liquid large-caps. Expect few/none in a large-cap band; point the universe at small caps (min_cap_m 300, max_cap_m 5000) to surface them.

Episodic Pivot earnings gap

KeyMeaning
ep_entry_timeET time the EP open-scan runs (default "09:40" — after the first 5-min bar).
ep_gapMin opening gap % (today's open vs prev close), default 10.
ep_max_priorMax prior-6-month gain % so the earnings gap is the fresh catalyst (default 50).
ep_or_barsNumber of 5-min bars in the opening range (1 = first 5 min; 3 = 15 min; 12 = 60 min).
ep_min_vol_fracMin intraday volume-so-far as a fraction of the 20-day avg daily volume (default 0.05).
ep_min_surpriseMin earnings surprise % to require (0 = off). Uses the Nasdaq calendar's surprise field.
EP runs at the open. It confirms the earnings catalyst from the Nasdaq calendar (prev-day after-hours + today pre-market), then places a buy-stop at the opening-range high. It has its own schedule slot (ep_entry_time), separate from the breakout/parabolic daily scan.

Exits & sizing

KeyMeaning
risk_per_trade_pctFraction of capital risked per ticker (default 0.01 = 1%).
max_positionsPer-strategy cap (default 10). Breakout, EP, and parabolic_long each get their OWN 10 slots at 1% risk — so up to 30 names total if all three run. A symbol already held/pending in any strategy is never entered twice. If a strategy's scan is empty, it's skipped.
quality_weightsOptional override of the breakout ranking weights (keys: rs, adr, tight, voldry, near, trend, hl). Merges over the defaults — omit to use them.
max_position_pctCap on any single position as a fraction of capital (default 0.30 — his overnight max).
partial_daysDays held before the partial sell (default 5; his "3–5 days").
partial_fractionFraction sold at the partial (default 0.5 = half). Stop then moves to break-even.
trail_smaMoving average the runner trails (10 or 20). Exit on first daily close below it.

Schedule

KeyMeaning
ep_entry_timeET time the EP earnings-gap open-scan runs (default "09:40"). Only used if "ep" is in setups.
entry_timeET time the daily breakout/parabolic scan runs (default "09:40", after the opening range).
entry_window[start, end] ET — entries + EP fire ONLY inside this window (default ["09:40","11:00"]). Lets you run the bot 24/7: starting it after hours (or mid-afternoon) places no late/after-hours entry orders — it just waits for the next window. Management + reconnect stay always-on.
manage_timeET time the daily management pass runs (default "15:50", near the close).
poll_secondsHeartbeat interval between the two daily jobs (default 60).
entry_tifTime-in-force for the buy-stop entry (default "DAY"; child protective stop is GTC).
