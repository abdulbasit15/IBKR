Method

  • Data: Historical Data/data/{SYM}_cont_5mins_rth.csv + _5m_recent.csv (IB continuous futures, RTH), sessions from 2025-09-02 (older rows in those files are forward-fill padded). Bars 09:30–15:55 ET.
  • Entries on the bar close → next bar open (+1 tick) or a stop-entry order; stop-market exits (+1 tick); 2R limit target from the actual fill; same-bar stop/target → stop; commission NQ $2.25, ES $2.25, GC $2.42, YM $2.25, CL $2.35 per side.
  • Portfolio rules (same as the bot): one position per symbol (earlier entry wins, then strategy_priority), max_risk_usd per symbol, max_trades_per_day, daily_loss_limit_usd.
  • Research behind the selection: scalping-v1/RR2_Strategy_Report.md and Windows_Report.md (18 strategy families, 160+ configs, time-window study).
