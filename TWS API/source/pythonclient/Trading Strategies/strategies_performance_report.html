Ranking — top to bottom

Weighted for edge strength and robustness (sample length, out-of-sample behavior, whether costs are included).

  2. Qullamaggie — Pullback (continuation) — same 4.4-yr multi-regime basis, PF 1.52, best RF among the stock setups (3.5), and positive & accelerating in 2024/25/26. Not in the live bot config — worth adding.
  3. Scalping — ORB 2nd Break — best PF (2.65) and Sharpe (3.11) with slippage included, tiny 4.9% DD. Dinged only for the short, selection-biased 13-month sample.
  4. Supertrend — MGC 5m — standout of the ST family (PF 1.61, RF 14.6) and the one instrument that stayed positive even in the with-costs check. Frictionless headline inflates it.
  5. Scalping — H2/L2 Trend Pullback — PF 1.70, hits its 2R target 41% of the time, and OOS PF improved (1.66→1.77).
  6. Scalping — Confluence Pullback — thin per-trade edge (+0.09R) but high volume, positive OOS (1.09), low DD.
  7. Supertrend — MES 5m — PF 1.38 frictionless, RF 6.4; costs pull it toward ~1.06–1.13.
  8. Supertrend — MNQ 5m (ST+DEMA) — PF 1.29 frictionless and weakest under costs (plain-ST MNQ failed both data halves, PF→0.95). DEMA kept for lower DD, not more profit.
  9. Scalping — IB Trend Breakout — RF ≈ 1.0, the NQ leg loses money (PF 0.92), OOS PF 0.96. Weak leg of the scalping portfolio.

Caveats behind the ranking

  • Costs flip conclusions. Supertrend's frictionless PFs (1.29–1.61) drop toward ~1.0 once commission + slippage are applied (reality-check: MNQ 1.10 / MES 1.13 / MGC 1.02, Sharpe 0.69 / 0.90 / 0.16). Scalping-v2's figures already include slippage, so it is judged on a harder standard.
  • Sample length is wildly uneven. Supertrend and scalping cover ~1–1.5 yr (one regime); only Qullamaggie spans multiple regimes — and even there, 2024 dominates (combined +53.9% that year vs losses in 3 of the other 4).
  • Qullamaggie's universe is survivorship-biased (today's winners), so live numbers would be lower. Scalping-v2's own stock cross-check warned its edge "largely vanished" in the unseen 2024–25 year.
  • Qullamaggie drawdown is approximate — computed on realized P&L in exit-date order from a $100k base; it ignores open-position mark-to-market and uses 0.5% risk (live bot uses 1%, so live $ P&L and DD would be roughly double).
